# Interactive Scene Archive

The site includes three independent Three.js cycling prototypes and their SVG studies. They are an artifact archive, not a controlled model benchmark. SVG thumbnails are illustrations rather than screenshots of the 3D scenes.

| Prototype | Files | Three.js |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Frontier | `public/experiments/pelican/frontier/` | 0.160.1 |
| Codex | `public/experiments/pelican/codex/` | 0.170.0 |
| WorkBuddy | `public/experiments/pelican/workbuddy/` | 0.160.1 |

Three.js modules are stored locally in each `vendor/` directory, with their MIT license and original copyright notices. The corresponding upstream project is [mrdoob/three.js](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js). External font dependencies were replaced with system fonts in the Codex prototype. Localizing dependencies does not establish authorship of the underlying library.

The site also redistributes 34 chapters from [HowToLiveBetter](https://github.com/eternity4719/HowToLiveBetter), snapshot `91a4f53` dated 2026-10-04. Text is under CC BY 4.0. Each guide page retains attribution, links to the source and license, and an explanation of the formatting and link changes. The guide is a historical snapshot, not a newly verified medical or legal reference.

Internal source locations and chat identifiers are excluded from this public snapshot. Research project pages contain high-level summaries only; private manuscripts, research source archives, datasets, weights, and internal experiment tables are not distributed.
